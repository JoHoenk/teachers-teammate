Model files (Apache-2.0)
=======================

The built-in reader and the automatic image preparation run neural network models published by
PaddlePaddle under the Apache License, Version 2.0: PP-OCRv6 small text detection and recognition,
PP-LCNet_x1_0_doc_ori (page orientation) and UVDoc (flattening of photographed pages), in their
ONNX exports on huggingface.co/PaddlePaddle.  They are bundled with the installed app
(teachers_teammate/_models/), fetched at build time at a pinned revision and checked against a
SHA-256 sum (see teachers_teammate/domain/local_models.py).  A build that does not carry them
downloads each once, only when the user asks for it.
